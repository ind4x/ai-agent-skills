# Design & UI/UX Engineering

Skills for high-craft UI/UX, design systems, frontend styling, typography, animations, optical alignment, and eliminating AI aesthetic clichés.

---

## Category Principles & Scope

- High-craft frontend execution: concentric border radii, subtle elevation shadows, optical alignment, and balanced text wrapping.
- Elimination of generic AI design slop: removal of unnecessary badges, synthetic gradients, decorative noise, and low-contrast palettes.
- Systematic design token integration with curated palettes (e.g., Sanzō Wada dictionary) and strict accessibility standards.
